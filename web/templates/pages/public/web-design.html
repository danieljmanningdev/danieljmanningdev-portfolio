01 / What I design

A strong website should make the business easier to understand and the user's next decision easier to make.

02 / Principles

Premium, not precious.

The visual system should feel considered without getting in the way. Good design gives content hierarchy, reinforces trust and survives real content on real screens.

Hierarchy

Direct attention.

Use scale, spacing and contrast so people can understand the page before they read every word.

Accessibility

Design for use.

Readable typography, keyboard-friendly interaction and clear states are part of the design rather than a final checklist.

Consistency

Build a system.

Reusable patterns make the interface easier to learn and the website easier to extend later.

03 / Process

From rough idea to coherent interface.

I work from the problem outward: understand the content and users, structure the experience, establish the visual language, then refine it against the browser.

01

03

04

Refine

Test the real implementation and remove anything that makes the experience less clear.
